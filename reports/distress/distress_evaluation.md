## Target and data

- **Distress** in a fiscal year: (net loss and negative operating cash flow) or negative equity or operating income below interest expense (interest > 0.5% of assets).
- **Target:** distress in the company's next fiscal year (within 12 months).
- **Data:** 46947 company-years, 7101 US companies, fiscal years 2010–2025 (SEC EDGAR frames; operating companies with revenue, banks/insurers and shells excluded).
- **Base rate:** 34.8% of company-years are followed by distress; 78.4% of companies in distress stay in distress.
- **Timing:** a company-year is usable 90 days after its fiscal year end (10-K deadline); training uses only labels known by then.

## Limitations

- US SEC filers only; scoring Indian or other companies uses Yahoo statements, whose definitions differ slightly, and is outside the training population.
- Frames hold the latest filed value, so a few figures may be restated.
- Distress here is an accounting definition (losses with cash burn, negative equity, weak interest cover), not bankruptcy or default.
- Scores are not calibrated probabilities; class weights are balanced.
